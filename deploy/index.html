Setup Guide

Follow these steps carefully to deploy your bot.

Step-by-Step Guide

01. Get Session: Visit gestonmd.tech/pairing, link your account, and copy your Session ID.

02. Upload: Upload the downloaded ZIP file to your panel's File Manager.

03. Extract: Right-click the ZIP and select Unarchive or Extract.

04. Session Setup: Locate session.json, open it, and paste your Session ID inside.

05. Configure: Open settings.js to adjust your bot settings and owner info.

06. Start: Go to your console and hit Run/Start to activate your bot!
